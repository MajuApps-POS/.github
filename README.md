# MajuApps

MajuApps builds operating systems for growing businesses in Indonesia.

## Products

- **MajuApps FnB**: POS, cashier shifts, receipts, and daily sales operations.
- **MajuApps Retail**: store sales, catalog, customer, and outlet operations.
- **MajuApps Inventory**: warehouse, stock movement, transfer, opname, and audit.

## Focus

- Reliable POS workflows
- Multi-outlet operations
- Stock and inventory control
- Business reports for owners
- Platform infrastructure for small teams that grow

## Repositories

- `majuapps-platform`: main application platform
- `majuapps-marketing`: public website and product pages
- `majuapps-ops`: deployment, operations, and runbooks
- `.github`: organization standards and profile
